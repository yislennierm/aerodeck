Flight now gives useful instrument data more of the480x272 screen. The visible
attitude area is232x182 instead of184x122:88.1% more area. The full native HUD
field is324x182 at(78,62), centered on(240,153). Aircraft, zero-pitch horizon and
both primary tape readouts share that datum. True10-degree pitch marks retain
the native1.85px/degree scale. Ground-speed ticks remain nonnegative.
Compact live P/R readouts show signed pitch/roll degrees in the upper corners.
They replace the bank arc so guide marks do not cross pitch captions. Missing
attitude does not produce synthetic angles.

replaced on Flight by the heading ribbon and a compact shield control. Touch
the shield to open Arm Stops. With no current focus, press ENTER once to focus
the shield and again to open its read-only reasons page; RTN returns to Flight.
Other pages retain their context tabs and controls. No FC arming command is sent.

retain the native29px MIDSIZE font. Link quality, RSSI and TX power remain on
the left, with actual SNR telemetry in dB below. The right rail retains pack/
cell voltage and remaining, adds current in its own block, and keeps used mAh.
Remaining estimated from capacity retains its ~ prefix. Missing values stay --.

The normal Flight footer now contains only flight mode, local-home distance,
satellite count and MSP status. It no longer repeats the side-panel LQ, RSSI or
pack voltage. Existing HOME/current body cards are removed to extend the HUD.
Battery/link/no-telemetry warnings still replace the full26px footer and retain
their detail action. Satellite count is not a GPS fix-type claim; local home
does not alter the FC home. Other pages keep their existing status footer.

No new runtime image is added. Attitude uses the native HUD primitive and
existing cached icons. Telemetry sources, freshness, pitch convention, read-only
MSP scheduling, settings formats and the full/compact widget are unchanged.
