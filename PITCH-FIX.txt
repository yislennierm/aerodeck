AERODECK — PITCH-FIX-20261016
INSTALLED AND VERIFIED: 2026-10-16T19:05:59.018419+02:00; safely ejected.

Confirmed bug: CRSF Ptch in radians was converted with the same positive sign
as Roll. Original Toolbox2.0 instead returns -math.deg(Ptch) for radian pitch.
AERODECK now applies that pitch-only correction once in shared telemetry.
The Flight horizon, pitch ladder, numeric values and Video inset all agree.
Degree-valued pitch retains its convention; Roll/Yaw/Heading are unchanged.
Level pitch is normalized to positive zero. No scheduling or MSP changes.

Expected at level Roll: nose up moves the horizon down and shows more sky;
nose down moves it up and shows more ground. The native EdgeTX renderer and
our pitch ladder both use 1.85 pixels per degree. This is a sign fix.

Verification: nine portable suites, including54 pitch sign/unit/source/stale
checks and both real app renderers at -20/0/+20 degree raw pitch. Regression
failed on the old conversion and passes on the corrected source. Synthetic
native previews are separate from a physical-quad pose/performance check.

Original Toolbox bytecode verified: GYRO pitch callback multiplies math.deg
by -1; TLMUPDS calls that callback; AHR forwards corrected pitch to X.HR;
X.HR is lcd.drawHudRectangle. The recovered TLMUPDS Lua omits the callback,
so the original bytecode was checked. GYRO.luac SHA256:
23095d5121f1c016aa79d7e64348ca57083a0cdb9434448e83762dbf472c5eee
