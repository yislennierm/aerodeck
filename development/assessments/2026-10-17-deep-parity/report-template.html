New concrete AERODECK defects

Bat% can show a calculated value as if raw. With reported 91%, 300 mAh used and a 1500 mAh profile, Telemetry shows 80%.

The duplicate flightmode declaration leaves Model Setup’s sensor picker detached from refresh. Home/track acquisition is also owned by GPS page rendering.

Reference behavior to improve

Toolbox’s CRSF “fix/HDOP” is a satellite-count heuristic. Its PIT-off branch and error-log toggle appear defective in original bytecode.

Copy functional outcomes, then verify them. Do not reproduce false quality labels or damaged decompiled branches.

Both horizons use EdgeTX’s native renderer

Toolbox’s X.HR aliases lcd.drawHudRectangle. AERODECK calls the same API. The meaningful differences are the overlays, sources, clipping, cues and lifecycle—not “images versus lines” as a blanket comparison.
