This revision refines the Flight data hierarchy without changing its geometry.
It is designed for the user's reported 30–40 cm viewing distance on the TX16S
MkII. Native font sizes are measured; physical readability has not been measured.

The right rail gives pack voltage, consumed mAh and current the primary 29 px
value slots. Consumed capacity moves out of the small bottom row. Derived
AVG CELL V moves to the 17 px bottom slot: it is pack voltage divided by the
configured cell count, not telemetry from individual cells. The left rail
prioritizes LQ, RSSI and SNR at 29 px; TX power moves to the 17 px bottom slot.
Long values use the existing measured fitting, so not every digit count remains
29 px. Missing values stay --, never synthetic zeroes.

The remaining-percentage label keeps its ~ prefix when estimated. Its bar
shrinks when needed to fit the label, including ~100%; the label sits above
the separator. Missing-data icons are muted. The LQ icon follows the configured
critical/low thresholds, and the battery icon is orange during a battery warning.

The visible attitude field remains 232 × 180, with full HUD (78, 64, 324, 180)
and shared zero (240, 154). No attitude area is gained or lost in this revision.
The 70 px rails, six navigation icons, header, compass, aircraft datum, tapes,
ARM INFO control and full 26 px warning banners retain their geometry.
Flight's unique-data footer keeps FM, app-local home distance, satellite count
and MSP status; it does not duplicate the link or battery values. A satellite
count is not a GPS-fix claim, and app-local home is not FC home.

Battery boundary fix: per-cell voltage is rounded to 0.1 mV before comparison
with the existing saved 0.1 V thresholds. This prevents native float32 roundoff
from falsely flagging an exact full pack, such as 6S at 25.2 V with a 4.2 V
maximum, as ABOVE MAXIMUM. Saved settings and the alarm policy are unchanged;
the boundary classification is corrected.

Attitude sampling, telemetry freshness rules, pitch convention, read-only MSP
scheduling, widget behavior and runtime asset files are unchanged. No new rates,
transport requests, runtime SD writes or FC/RF/model/output writes are added.
