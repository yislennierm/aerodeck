Radio remains GPS-UNIFIED-20261018 (see DEPLOYMENT.txt).

The former default Dark palette is now named TX16S (same saved theme=0).
Green #00FF00: healthy status, radio battery gauge, default active underline,
link/capacity gauges and selection emphasis. Blue #0000FF: selected toolbar
backplate, with white foreground. Light blue #70A8FF: readable controls,
keyboard focus, GPS track and instrument accents against the dark background.
The two pure RGB values are nominal LED-channel equivalents, not measured
physical LED colors. The lighter foreground avoids pure blue text on black.

Setup > User interface > Display palette: TX16S. Default Highlight colour:
Green. The former Cyan highlight option is now Blue; custom highlight selection
continues to work. Health/shield colors remain independent of custom highlights.
Saved Dark selections adopt TX16S without rewriting settings. Daylight, Night
and High contrast remain available. Warning/error amber/red and conventional
sky/ground colors retain their meaning.

Rendering: cached palette, existing icons, same draw count, no new runtime
assets, image decodes or animations. Existing telemetry/attitude loops untouched.

not measure physical radio performance or actual LCD-to-LED color matching.
